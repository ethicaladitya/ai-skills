# Legal content for India

## Accuracy
- Every judgment gets its case name, court, date and citation or case number, plus a link to the
  judgment (the product's case page or an official source). Quote the holding accurately; paraphrase
  the reasoning and label it as your reading.
- **Never state inferred metadata as fact** (bench composition, decision date, court). A wrong bench
  is a false statement about a court record. If a detail isn't in the judgment header, leave it out.
- Statutes: name the section and Act precisely (e.g. "Order XXVI Rule 9, Code of Civil Procedure, 1908").
- Add a one-line "This is general information, not legal advice" note on explainers and guides.

## Process guides
Walk the official process on the official site (eCourts, sci.gov.in, High Court portals) on the day
you write, and state that date. Show the manual path first, then any faster tool path. Screenshots
or exact field names beat descriptions.

## Comparisons with other legal research tools
- **Verify every claim about a competitor on their own site, that day,** and write "as of <month year>".
- Say where the competitor is stronger. Readers trust fair comparisons, and they get cited.
- Disclose who publishes the comparison. No disparagement, no logos, no invented prices or features.
- Compare on what users choose by: coverage (courts, years), search quality, citations and citators,
  summaries, AI features, price and free tier, speed, export and citation formats.

## Community and opportunity posts
Only real, currently open calls (moot courts, papers, essays, internships), with deadline,
eligibility and the official link. Remove or mark them closed when they expire.

# Authority building (white-hat only)

## Hard lines
No buying or selling links, link exchanges or networks, comment/forum/wiki spam, fake or gated reviews,
or mass low-quality guest posts. They break search engine spam policies and can cost a site its index.
Outreach is sent by a real person from their own account. Agents draft; people send.

## Order of work
1. **Destinations first.** Links and mentions should land on pages that deserve them and convert:
   service or product pages, comparison and alternatives pages, a strong About or author page, linkable
   assets. Fix the pages before asking anyone to link to them.
2. **Entity basics.** The same name, bio and description everywhere, with `sameAs` links in schema. A
   press or about page with a one-paragraph description, bios, logos, screenshots and a direct contact email.
3. **Relevant directories and profiles.** Only where the business genuinely fits:
   - B2B service agencies: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, Google Business Profile, Bing Places.
   - Software or tools: product and alternatives directories, integration marketplaces.
   - People: GitHub, WordPress.org, speaker pages, community profiles.
   - Vary the description per directory (lead with what that audience cares about), keep NAP identical,
     and log each listing (URL, date, status, whether the link is live).
4. **Reviews on the platforms buyers use,** asked of real clients with a direct link; never gated.
5. **Linkable assets:** original data, useful checklists and cheat sheets, free tools, honest comparisons.
   These earn links without asking.
6. **Digital PR,** in several modes:
   - **Inbound:** answer journalist requests (Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer) fast, in under 200 words.
   - **Reactive:** add a sharp, expert angle to trending news within hours.
   - **Proactive:** pitch real stories (data, a strong opinion, a milestone, a client's before and after).
   - **Owned:** the press page above.
7. **Mention reclamation:** find unlinked mentions of the brand or person and politely ask for a link.
8. **Community and talks:** conference and meetup talks with slides and a recap post, and genuine,
   helpful participation where the audience is. Answer; don't advertise.

## Pitch quality bar (every answer must be yes, or don't send)
Does the recipient cover this topic (check their last 5 pieces)? Is there a clear hook? Could they write the
story from this email alone (data, quote, contact)? Is the subject line specific? Is it under 150 words?
Is it free of hype words? Is the ask clear?

## Measure
Live links and their quality, referral traffic, brand-search growth, citations in AI answers, and leads
that mention the coverage. Ignore vanity metrics.
